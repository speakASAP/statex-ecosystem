# Vision

completeness_level: complete

## One-sentence vision
Create a low-priority, truthful ecosystem catalog that helps teams discover Statex services and operational metadata without overstating the application’s business criticality.

## Problem statement
The ecosystem is difficult to navigate without a consistent catalog of repos, services, URLs, and metadata. The repo should help operators and engineers find the right service without claiming ownership of the runtime workflows behind those entries.

## Target users
- Platform operators and engineers navigating the Statex ecosystem
- Service maintainers who need a compact directory of app metadata and URLs
- Decision-makers who need a clear map of the ecosystem without inflating app priority

## Core user need
Teams need a simple, truthful catalog that keeps service metadata discoverable while preserving the proper ownership boundaries of the underlying repos.

## Key outcomes
- Service metadata and route information remain accurate and easy to navigate
- The catalog improves ecosystem discoverability without absorbing service runtime ownership
- The low-priority status remains explicit and reviewable in the repository docs

## Non-goals
- Running core business workflows or product-critical domain logic
- Owning the runtime responsibilities of the service repos it links to
- Turning the catalog into a higher-priority product application without a real scope change

## Success criteria
- The app remains useful as a low-priority catalog and service index
- The repo’s boundary stays truthful in onboarding and operational documentation
- The catalog remains easy to maintain without inventing product-critical claims
